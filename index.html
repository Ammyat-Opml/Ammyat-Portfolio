About

A little about me

I’m a Mechatronics Engineering student at the University of Waterloo, and I really like building things you can hold. Most of my work so far has been on the mechanical side: robotics, jigs and fixtures, and capstone mechanism work. I’m now learning to layer control systems, embedded programming, and sensor-driven autonomy on top of that, which is what brought me to Mechatronics in the first place.

I’m looking for co-op and internship opportunities in mechanical design, tool design, robotics, and mechatronic systems. I’d love a place where I can keep learning and ship hardware end to end.
